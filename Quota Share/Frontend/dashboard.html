    📌 Key Treaty Takeaways

    Proportional Dual-Leg Flow

    Quota Share proportionally divides both top-line gross premium and incurred claims identically according to the treaty percentage.

    Risk Transfer vs Exposure

    Higher QS provides robust balance sheet solvency relief during catastrophe events, but relinquishes retained underwriting margin.

    Commission Economic Rebate

    Ceding commission shifts economic cash flow back to the primary insurer to reimburse upfront acquisition and administration costs.

    Strategic Trade-offs

    No reinsurance structure is universally optimal: the right QS depends on capital adequacy, risk appetite, pricing accuracy, and market cycle.

    Learning Note: Quota Share changes how premium, claims, and commissions are shared proportionally. Whether a higher or lower QS is preferable depends on the primary insurer's risk appetite, capital solvency requirements, pricing accuracy, and overall treaty expense terms.
